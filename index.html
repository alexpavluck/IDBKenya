Kenya Last-Mile Treatment Dashboard

Soil-transmitted helminthiases (STH) and schistosomiasis (SCH) mass drug administration, reported by ward and aggregated to sub-county and county. Source extract through 27 July 2026.

Read before interpreting. This feed covers STH and SCH only (no LF, oncho, or trachoma) and carries no target population, so figures are treatments delivered, not coverage. Counts are treatments, not unique people: a person treated in successive rounds is counted each time. This extract adds a unique event id, so the multiple rows per ward, date, and drug (up to 16) are confirmed distinct events rather than duplicates, though the sub-ward dimension separating them is still unlabeled. Wards are placed by their DHIS2 org-unit id, which resolves the earlier same-name ambiguity; 99.5% of treatments are placed, with two unmatched wards listed under Data quality.
